# Plan: Self-Healing & Auto-Scaling Infrastructure

**Requirements:** HEAL-01 (Failure Detection & Recovery), HEAL-02 (Auto-Scaling)
**Wave:** 1
**Priority:** P0

---

## Context

Phase 1.2 requirements HEAL-01 and HEAL-02 focus on autonomous failure recovery and load-based scaling. The research identified that key infrastructure already exists but needs wiring:

- **HEAL-01:** `_handle_agent_failure()` in steward.py only logs failures — doesn't trigger restart. `ActorSupervisor._attempt_restart()` exists but is never called from the monitoring loop.
- **HEAL-02:** `AgentPoolManager` in scaling.py has full infrastructure. Missing: queue depth/latency triggers integration, scale-down graceful drain.

**Key files:**
- `src/heretek_swarm/actors/steward.py` (lines 395-425) — monitor loop + failure handling
- `src/heretek_swarm/actors/supervisor.py` (lines 337-394) — restart logic
- `src/heretek_swarm/runtime/scaling.py` (lines 59-84) — ScalingConfig dataclass
- `src/heretek_swarm/runtime/autonomous_runtime.py` (lines 334-388) — scale methods
